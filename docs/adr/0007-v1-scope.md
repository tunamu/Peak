# 0007. v1 includes widgets, Live Activity, HealthKit workouts and iCloud sync

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-29

## Context

These features could be deferred, but they are part of the intended daily use.

## Decision

Ship widgets, a Live Activity, writing workouts to HealthKit and iCloud sync in v1.

## Consequences

- v1 needs App Groups, WidgetKit, ActivityKit and CloudKit, and therefore a paid Apple Developer Program membership.
