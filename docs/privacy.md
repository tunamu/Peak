# Peak Privacy Policy

> Status: draft. Reviewed and published on GitHub Pages before the App Store release (F12).

Peak is built so that your data stays yours.

## What Peak does not do

- No accounts, no sign-up.
- No servers run by Peak. Your data is never sent to the developer.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device**, in the app's local database.
- **In your own iCloud account**, only if you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings (it is off until you do), so your data
  syncs between your devices. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.
- **Delete All Data** in Settings deletes everything Peak stores, after saving a backup file on your device (Files ›
  Peak › Backups) that you can import again or delete. With iCloud Sync on, it also deletes the data from iCloud.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Peak reads steps, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and water intake to show
your daily metrics and Energy Level. It writes water intake and completed workouts to Apple Health.

Data read from Apple Health stays on your device. It is never stored in iCloud, never sent anywhere and never used for
advertising. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Energy Level is an estimate to help plan training. It is not medical advice.

## Import and export

Files you import are read on your device. Exports are files you create and share yourself.
